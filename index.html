01 Profile

The two projects below explain me better than adjectives can: a 49-issue magazine built over a year of evenings and weekends, and a pirate radio station built to win this job, 107 assets produced across three months and broadcast over three weeks. The pattern behind both is the same. Constant experimentation, a genuine love of the technology, and a flat refusal to let the work plateau: every asset a module in a compounding system, so the hundredth piece embarrasses the first. Six years in tech marketing include two and a half inside the Mina ecosystem at Mina Foundation and o1Labs, where I learned ZK first-hand from the engineers behind the Kimchi proof system. Not the deep maths, but their thinking and their vision, and it convinced me to point my career at this technology. My philosophy is Seth Godin's: make work worth remarking on, because ideas spread person to person and word of mouth carries trust no channel can. AI has been in my workflow since early 2022, and I study marketing constantly, mostly from outside crypto, because Web3 spends too much time looking at itself.
